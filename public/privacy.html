Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This policy applies to the animembient content publishing tool (the "App"), which uploads ambient music videos to YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram via official platform APIs.

1. Who we are

Operated by Oleksandr Kuznetsov, Ukraine. Contact: ytokuznetsov@gmail.com

2. Data collected

The App does not collect, store, or process personal data of any third party. It accesses only the authorized creator's TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram accounts via official OAuth APIs to upload video content.

3. OAuth tokens

Authentication tokens are stored locally on the creator's machine and are never shared with third parties.

4. Third-party platforms

Videos are published to TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram. Each platform's own privacy policy governs how they handle the uploaded content.

This policy may be updated occasionally. The latest version is always available at this URL.
