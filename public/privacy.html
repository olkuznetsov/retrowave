animembient Content Publishing Tool

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how animembient (the "App") collects, uses, and protects information when you connect your TikTok, YouTube, or Instagram account to the App via official platform APIs.

1. Who we are

animembient is operated by Oleksandr Kuznetsov, Ukraine.
Contact: ytokuznetsov@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

When you authorize the App via TikTok Login Kit, we receive the following data from TikTok's API:

  • Open ID — a unique identifier for your TikTok account
  • Display name — your public TikTok username
  • Avatar URL — a link to your TikTok profile picture

This data is accessed via the user.info.basic scope solely to verify that you are the authorized account owner before uploading content.

The App does not collect, read, or store any of the following:

  • Your TikTok followers, following list, or engagement data
  • Your TikTok messages or private content
  • Personal data of any TikTok user other than the authorized creator
  • Payment or financial information

3. How we use your information

The information obtained from TikTok is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate you as the authorized creator before uploading video content
  • Upload video content to your TikTok account as a draft via the Content Posting API (video.upload scope)

Your data is never used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than operating the upload feature you explicitly requested.

4. Data storage and security

OAuth access tokens and the basic profile data listed in Section 2 are stored locally on the creator's own machine in a configuration file. This data is never transmitted to any server controlled by animembient, and is never shared with third parties.

Access is protected by your operating system's file permissions. You are responsible for securing your own machine.

5. Data retention and deletion

Data is retained only as long as you choose to use the App. To delete all stored data, simply remove the App's configuration files from your machine (the tiktok_token.json, youtube_token.json, and instagram_token.json files in the App directory). Revoking access in your TikTok account settings (Settings → Security → Manage App Permissions) will also immediately invalidate any stored tokens.

6. Third-party platforms

The App interacts with TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram via their official APIs. Each platform's own privacy policy governs how they handle data on their side:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Google / YouTube Privacy Policy
  • Instagram / Meta Privacy Policy

7. Children's privacy

The App is intended for use by content creators and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Your rights

As the sole user of this App, you have full control over your data. You may at any time:

  • Revoke the App's access to your TikTok account via TikTok's app permission settings
  • Delete all locally stored tokens and profile data by removing the App's config files
  • Contact us at ytokuznetsov@gmail.com with any privacy-related questions or requests

This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes in the App or applicable law. The latest version is always available at this URL. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
